Questions people
ask first

Can Hamyon see my money? Is it safe?

Hamyon doesn’t connect to your bank and can’t charge or transfer anything. It only reads the SMS your bank has already sent to your phone, and keeps transactions on the phone itself.

How does Hamyon get my SMS?

iOS doesn’t let apps read messages directly. So you set up one automation in Shortcuts: bank SMS → Hamyon. It takes a couple of minutes, and then everything runs by itself.

Which banks are supported?

Any bank that sends purchase SMS: Aloqabank, SQB, Kapitalbank, TBC Bank, Hamkorbank, Uzum Bank and others. Older transactions can be added from screenshots or a PDF statement.

Do I need to type anything in?

No. Card purchases arrive on their own. Cash is easiest to add by voice: “lunch, 45 thousand”.

What does the AI do?

It reads SMS, sets categories, spots transfers between your own cards, answers questions about your spending and once a month suggests where you could save.

How do I split a bill with friends?

“Split the bill” works out shares equally or by what each person ordered, tip included. Debts are saved, and Hamyon reminds you when they’re due.

How much does it cost?

Hamyon is in beta and free for now.

Is there an Android version?

iPhone only for now.
